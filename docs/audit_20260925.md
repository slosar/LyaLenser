# Paper/code consistency corrections, 25 September 2026

The audit fixes validation and diagnostic code and describes the implemented
measurement accurately. It does not change the fiducial 55-component amplitude
fit, its input correlation tables, or the headline result in `joint_fit_v4.json`.
All manuscript text changes are marked with `\codex{...}`.

## Implementation changes

- `shift_positions` preserves per-pixel A/B labels. Previously the returned
  `SightlineSet` defaulted every pixel to A, admitting B×B pairs in auto-injection
  tests. A regression test checks that even a zero shift preserves their exclusion.
- Injection slopes now have paired jackknife errors, matching region IDs across
  shifts and retaining the covariance of positive and negative injections. Curl
  slope errors use the jackknife of the actual mean curl statistic.
- Scale sensitivity includes the same source-distance expansion and boundary
  selection as the production response. The script checks its summed cell density
  against the independent cached-accumulator contraction for each statistic;
  regression tests cover auto and cross pairs and a radial-boundary pixel.
- The standalone null driver reuses the saved production pair catalogues, stores
  seeds and individual amplitudes/errors, and checkpoints each realization. Its
  memory-efficient region partials solve the same combined-template model as the
  original null driver. This is an 11-component diagnostic, not a randomization of
  all 55 fiducial slice components.

## Manuscript corrections

- The cross model inherits the **base-only** auto parameters. Its four physical
  parameters are fitted first, then held fixed when fitting the spline and its
  evolution. Its quoted degrees of freedom are conditional on that base fit.
- Bias fitting uses nonlinear Halofit matter power with a linear tracer bias.
- CMB deflection errors use smooth auto-spectrum models derived from convergence
  measurements and the predicted cross-spectrum.
- Masked coverage-class filtering is an approximate Wiener construction. The
  exact covariance identity does not prove its normalization; the CMB comparison
  provides an empirical check, and injecting the template itself is not an
  independent physical calibration.
- The common source-distance correction is acknowledged; the information plot
  is explicitly the **unmarginalised** response, not the full covariance-weighted
  information after nuisance fitting.
- The largest response off-diagonal connects junk and L=200–400 in the highest
  slice, not neighbouring science windows.
- The average sample density and Lyα–Lyβ comoving separation are corrected.
- The kernel-error appendix uses a consistent derivative definition and states
  the assumptions behind its attenuation estimate; increasing the quality of the
  correlation measurement can reduce kernel noise.
- Two missing bibliography entries were restored from the repository's existing
  ADS export, with marked titles.

## 100-realization null outcome

The enlarged sample does not support a persistent null offset. Errors on means
below are Monte Carlo standard errors, not the errors of a single measurement.

| Statistic | Mean ± SEM | Realization scatter | RMS jackknife error | Scatter / RMS JK (bootstrap 95%) |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Auto | −0.02226 ± 0.04462 | 0.44618 | 0.44468 | 1.003 (0.883–1.110) |
| Cross | −0.05120 ± 0.06134 | 0.61335 | 0.58708 | 1.045 (0.894–1.183) |

The original 20 amplitudes reproduce to within 2.6e-8. The first 20 auto draws
gave −0.20249 ± 0.08819; the additional 80 give +0.02280 ± 0.05022. The full-sample
two-sided mean tests give p=0.619 (auto) and p=0.406 (cross). This is consistent
with a finite-sample fluctuation, not evidence for a persistent bias. The sign
reversal checks give exactly opposite amplitudes and unchanged uncertainties.
This is the expected algebra: reversing all template components reverses the
score and mean field but leaves the quadratic response unchanged. A symmetric
Gaussian template ensemble therefore has zero mean amplitude conditional on the
fixed data. The finite 20-draw mean does not by itself imply an estimator offset.
The bootstrap intervals concern this conditional random-template ensemble;
they do not establish coverage of the full 55-component science fit.

All individual seeds, amplitudes, errors and summaries are saved in
`results/random_template_null_100_v4.json`.

## Corrected injection outcome

| Statistic / mode | Paired science slope | Paired jackknife error |
|---|---:|---:|
| Auto, full-table expectation | 1.024141 | 0.000523 |
| Auto, lensable-only expectation (±0.25) | 0.990707 | 0.000402 |
| Auto, real data | 1.060013 | 0.040270 |
| Cross, expectation | 0.998107 | 0.000236 |
| Cross, real data | 0.997770 | 0.047931 |

The real-data mean-curl slopes are 0.04540 ± 0.06732 (auto) and
0.00624 ± 0.10174 (cross). Both science slopes are compatible with unity;
the auto slope differs from unity by 1.49 jackknife errors and from the
full-table expectation by 0.89 errors. The old auto slope was 1.058227, so
preserving the A/B selection changes the paired slope only slightly, although
it changes the underlying shifted amplitudes. The corrected noiseless auto
selection admits 209.66–209.75 billion pixel pairs over the four shifts.

Removing the same-wavelength term changes the expectation slope, but this
does not establish a decomposition of the real-data excess into instrumental
and other causes. Nor does it establish that the residual 0.9% lensable-only
deficit is caused by boundary crossings. Those attributions were removed.
These are geometric diagnostics, not an independent physical calibration;
the fiducial science amplitude is not divided by an injection slope.
All modes, paired slope samples, errors and region IDs are in
`results/injection_v4_corrected.json`, with the lensable-only run separately
in `results/injection_v4_corrected_nosw.json`.

## Corrected scale diagnostic

The corrected scale histogram matches the cached response to relative differences
of −4.5e-12 (auto) and −3.5e-9 (cross). Its transverse peak bins are centred at
6.5 and 12.5 Mpc/h; the medians remain 13 and 16 Mpc/h, so the rounded scale
statements in the paper are unchanged. The figure was regenerated from
`results/scale_sensitivity_v4_corrected.json`.

## Reproduction commands

Use the conda Python documented in `docs/computing.md` and `LYALENSER_DATA`.
`scripts/slurm/audit_validation.sbatch` contains the NERSC commands and independent
20-draw shards. For a workstation the same scripts run directly:

```bash
python scripts/random_template_null.py --statistic auto --start 0 --stop 100 --out /path/null_auto.json
python scripts/random_template_null.py --statistic cross --start 0 --stop 100 --out /path/null_cross.json
python scripts/injection_dr1.py --auto "$LYALENSER_DATA/stageb/dr1_lowz_v7d" --cross "$LYALENSER_DATA/stageb/dr1_qso_v1d" --lowz "$LYALENSER_DATA/lowz_v4" --bands 40 200 400 600 800 1000 --tag v4_corrected
python scripts/injection_dr1.py --auto "$LYALENSER_DATA/stageb/dr1_lowz_v7d" --lowz "$LYALENSER_DATA/lowz_v4" --bands 40 200 400 600 800 1000 --tag v4_corrected_nosw --remove-same-wavelength --modes expectation --amplitudes -0.25 0.25
python scripts/scale_sensitivity.py --bands 40 200 400 600 800 1000 --tag v4_corrected
```

The NERSC jobs use an isolated `LyaLenser_audit_20260925` copy, preserving existing
remote checkout changes. The local and NERSC forest files have identical SHA256:
`fc9a249b9a9a4e4e11098480312d97a97f737d019c5bc77d92cb782cda48fab2`.

The completed null ensemble combines local draws 0–99 (auto) and 0–59 (cross)
with NERSC job 58862646 tasks 8–9 (cross 60–99). The lensable-only injection
is job 58862641 task 11; the scale calculation is job 58862647 task 12.
The full injection was split by statistic/mode for the short queue: job
58863246 tasks 10 and 13 produce the auto expectation and both cross modes;
the auto real-data mode ran locally with 16 Numba threads. Mode shards are
merged without refitting or averaging their saved uncertainties:

```bash
python scripts/summarise_audit.py --directory "$LYALENSER_DATA/audit_20260925" \
  --injections results/injection_v4_corrected_auto_expectation.json \
               results/injection_v4_corrected_auto_real.json \
               results/injection_v4_corrected_cross.json
```

Validation: `NUMBA_NUM_THREADS=4 OMP_NUM_THREADS=2 OPENBLAS_NUM_THREADS=2
python -m pytest tests -q` passes all 54 tests. The original 20 nulls reproduce,
and both scale-response total assertions pass on the full catalogues.
